Participation journey

From application to completed trades.

Five steps take a buyer from first contact to regular participation. Each step depends on qualification and agreed terms.

  1. Step 1

    Tell us what you buy.

    Markets, device categories, models, conditions and purchasing capacity.

  2. Step 2

    Complete business and operational qualification.

    Company details, operating capabilities and the onboarding documents requested after the initial review.

  3. Step 3

    Agree commercial and operational responsibilities.

    Terms for offers, collection, inspection, discrepancies and settlement.

  4. Step 4

    Connect to suitable opportunities following approval.

    Review the device information and condition evidence available for each opportunity, then decide whether to make an offer.

  5. Step 5

    Build a performance record through completed transactions.

    Competitive offers, reliable execution and consistent settlement shape the relationship over time.

Fintegre's operational team confirms the final process, requirements and terms with each buyer.
