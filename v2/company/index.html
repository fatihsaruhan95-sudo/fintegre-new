What we
believe.

Four ideas that decide how we build the product and who we work with.

  • A used device is stock, not waste.

    Every phone in a drawer has a price. Our job is to find it and pay it out while it is still high.

  • The customer should see one number.

    Checked, valued and paid in under a minute. No forms, no waiting for a quote by email.

  • Buyers compete. We do not pick them.

    Each device goes to the best bid in its market, not to a fixed partner with a fixed price.

  • Your brand stays in front.

    We work behind your store, your site and your app. Customers never need to know our name.
