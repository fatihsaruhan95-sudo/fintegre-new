Our journey.

From affordability solutions to trade-in programs with retailers, telecom operators and e-commerce partners.

2017

Founded in Istanbul to provide affordability solutions.

2019

Launched our first trade-in counter in retail stores.

2020

Partnered with Apple to deliver a unified online experience.

2023

2025

2026

Surpassed one million devices checked across three regions.
