From pilot to rollout.

A pilot in selected stores first. Then the remaining stores and the online shop, on the same assessment flow and buyer rules. Timing depends on the agreed program.

Step 1Connect to checkoutTrade-in is integrated into checkout in stores and online. Staff use a guided device check.
Step 2Set up buyersQualified buyers are onboarded and submit offers under the agreed program rules.
Step 3Pilot in a few storesStaff, assessment and handover are tested with customers before rollout.
Step 4Roll outRemaining stores and the online shop use the same flow.

What to measure.

Agree the metric definitions before launch, so the pilot and the rollout are judged on the same basis.

01

Participation

Share of eligible device sales in which the customer completes a trade-in.

02

Value per device

Average customer credit per accepted device, tracked from quote to buyer settlement.

03

Time to offer

Time from the start of the device check to the offer shown to the customer.

04

Discrepancies

Devices where the buyer's inspection differs from the assessment made at the point of trade-in.
