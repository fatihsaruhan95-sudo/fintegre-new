# Brand essence & positioning

Fintegre is the B2B, white-label platform behind trade-in, buyback and upgrade programs.

## Rules

1. Speak to partners: retailers, telecom operators and device brands.
2. Never address consumers or promise them a price.
3. Let the partner's brand lead; Fintegre is white-label.
4. Never say or imply that Fintegre buys devices.
5. Use the promise word for word: “Make every device worth more.”
6. Back every claim with one real number.
7. Sound precise, calm, human and European.

## Specs

| | |
|---|---|
| Positioning | White-label trade-in intelligence and orchestration platform |
| Promise | Make every device worth more. |
| Audiences | Retailers · Telecom operators · Device brands (in this order) |
| Personality | Precise · Calm · Human · European |
| Programs | Trade-in, buyback, early upgrade, BYOD protection, warranty, claim validation, return avoidance |
