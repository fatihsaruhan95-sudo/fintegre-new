# Data & charts

Numbers you can check: one figure at a time, in real units and real places.

## Rules

1. Show one key number at a time: the value, its unit and one label.
2. Use real units: euros, seconds, percentages.
3. Use Baltic and Polish markets as examples; never real customer names.
4. Label bars directly at the bar end; skip legends where you can.
5. Highlight one bar or row in Indigo; keep the rest quiet.
6. Keep axes and gridlines faint, in Titanium.
7. Mark invented figures as “Example data”.

## Specs
