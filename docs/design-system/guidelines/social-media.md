# Social media

One idea per post, in the same rooms as the site, on a smaller screen.

## Rules

1. Give each post one idea, one number and one device or person.
2. Make the first line carry the idea, before “see more”.
3. Repeat the image headline in the caption, in plain words.
4. Use real numbers and neutral customer descriptors.
5. Keep 250 px free at the top and bottom of stories for the app UI.
6. Never add a row of hashtags; one or two at most.
7. Never put three numbers on a busy photo.
